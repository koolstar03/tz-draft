How to Play

Move: Click or drag pieces along dark diagonals.

Capture: Jump over an opponent piece. Captures are mandatory — if you can capture, you must. You may choose any capture route, but once you start a capture you must complete the full chain.

King: Reach the far back rank to promote. Kings move backward. When a piece becomes a king, its turn ends immediately — no further captures that turn.

Win: Capture all enemy pieces or block every move.

Draw: 3-fold repetition, or 40 moves without capture.

Keyboard: Tab/Arrows to navigate, Enter to select/piece, Space to confirm move.

Tanzanian Draft Rules

Captures are mandatory — if you can capture, you must.

Any legal capture route may be chosen. There is no max-capture rule.

Once a capture starts, the full chain must be completed.

Flying kings — kings move any distance diagonally, landing step-by-step after each capture.

Men cannot capture backward.

Promotion — when a man reaches the king row, the turn ends immediately. The newly promoted king does NOT continue capturing.
